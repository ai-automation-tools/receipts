Does the cited page actually say that?

Paste a claim and the text of the page it cites. Receipts finds the closest passage by shared words and figures and says whether it matches. This is the same matcher the browser extension runs, here in your browser: no model, no key, and nothing you paste leaves this page.

Try an example

Receipts only says whether the source contains the claim, never whether the claim is true. Its measured precision and recall are in the README.
